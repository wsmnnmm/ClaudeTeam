## 1) Hero Value Proposition

**Turn rough supplier photos into marketplace-ready product image packs in 24 hours.**
**Get white-background shots, lifestyle scenes, and ad creatives for Amazon, TikTok Shop, and Shopify—without booking a studio.**

### Target Visitor

- Primary: small cross-border sellers with 5-50 SKUs, sourcing from 1688/Yiwu/Shenzhen and listing on Amazon, TikTok Shop, or Shopify.
- Trigger: they have supplier photos or phone photos, but need clean marketplace assets before listing or ad testing.
- Core promise: replace the first expensive studio shoot with a fast AI-assisted image pack good enough for listing tests and ad experiments.
- Non-promise: not a final luxury brand photoshoot; transparent, reflective, jewelry, apparel fit, and regulated products may still need human photography or manual retouching.

## 2) Landing Page Structure

1. Hero: 24-hour image pack promise + upload CTA.
2. Pain: studio shoot costs thousands, delays launch, and each platform needs different images.
3. Demo Before/After: three SKU examples below.
4. Package: white-background + lifestyle + ad creatives + platform sizes.
5. Pricing Anchor: compare against studio shoot and manual designer workflow.
6. FAQ: quality, platform fit, source photo requirements, delivery time, refunds/revisions.
7. CTA: “Send 3 supplier photos, get a sample pack quote today.”

## 4) Pricing Anchor

### Suggested Launch Pricing

- Starter Sample: ¥199 for 1 SKU, 6 images, 24-48h delivery.
- Validation Pack: ¥699 for 3 SKUs, 24 images, includes white-background + lifestyle + ad variants.
- Store Launch Pack: ¥1,999 for 10 SKUs, 80 images, includes platform sizing and one revision round.

### Anchor Reason

- Traditional studio path: seller often expects several thousand RMB per shoot once photography, retouching, props, models/pets, and revisions are included.
- Manual designer path: cheaper than studio but still slow for SKU variants and platform sizes.
- AI-assisted path: price should feel like a low-risk listing/ad test budget, not a brand photoshoot budget.
- Validation target: if 3 of 10 contacted sellers accept ¥199-699 pilot pricing after seeing before/after samples, Product Lab can proceed to demo implementation.

## 5) FAQ — First 5 Questions

### Q1: What photos do I need to send?

Send 3-5 clear supplier or phone photos per SKU: front, side, close-up/detail, packaging if available, and color variants. Better input photos produce fewer AI mistakes.

### Q2: Can these images be used directly on Amazon or TikTok Shop?

They are designed for listing tests and ad creative tests, but final use depends on each platform’s current image policy and your product category. We include white-background and platform-size variants, but you should review before publishing.

### Q3: Will the AI change my product details?

We use your source photos as reference and run manual QA for obvious shape/color issues. Transparent materials, jewelry, fine texture, logos, and exact dimensions need extra checking or human retouching.

### Q4: How fast can I get the first pack?

For a pilot pack, target delivery is 24-48 hours after receiving usable source photos and SKU notes. Large SKU batches need a confirmed schedule.

### Q5: What if the result is not usable?

Start with the ¥199 sample pack before ordering a batch. If the sample exposes product-detail risks, we either revise once or recommend a traditional photo/retouch workflow for that SKU.
